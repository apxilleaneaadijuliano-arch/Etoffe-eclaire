Our Story

🇫🇷

Étoffe Éclairé means "Enlightened Fabric" in French. We chose a French name because France is celebrated worldwide for its timeless fashion, refinement, and style.

EC — our initials stand for "Étoffe Éclairé" → that's where "EC Tips & Tricks" comes from! Simple, practical advice to help you understand fabrics and build a wardrobe you truly love.

Get to Know Your Fabrics

Click each card to discover more ✨

☁️

Cotton

Natural +

Best for: Everyday wear, t-shirts, bedding

Feels like: Soft, gentle, breathable against skin

Care tip: Wash warm — high heat = shrinkage!

👑

Silk

Luxury +

Best for: Special occasions, blouses, scarves

Feels like: Smooth, cool, gently shiny

Care tip: Hand wash cold — treat it like royalty!

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic +

Best for: Travel, activewear, budget fashion

Feels like: Smooth, stretchy, wrinkle-resistant

Care tip: Machine wash — dries super fast!

🌿

Linen

Natural +

Best for: Summer elegance, dresses, shirts

Feels like: Crisp, textured, keeps you cool

Care tip: Wash cold — the wrinkles are part of its charm!

🐑

Wool

Natural +

Best for: Sweaters, coats, chilly days

Feels like: Warm, cozy, naturally springy

Care tip: Hand wash cold — lay flat to dry!

🌸

Rayon

Blend +

Best for: Dresses, blouses, flowy styles

Feels like: Silky, lightweight, beautifully soft

Care tip: Gentle wash — can shrink easily!

Questions You Might Have

Knowing fabrics saves you money, time, and wardrobe disappointments. You'll pick pieces that feel right, last longer, and stay beautiful. No more wasting money on items that fall apart after two washes!

Always check the description — look for percentages like "100% cotton" or "linen blend." If it just says "cotton feel" without details, it's likely synthetic. Message the seller — good ones will tell you!
